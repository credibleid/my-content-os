# Video Reels Engine Skill (Phase 2 Expansion)

This skill extends `my-content-os` into short-form vertical video production using the same brand profile and verified product knowledge base.

## Workflow

### 1. Script & Pacing Generation (33-Second Target)
- Ingest approved draft concept and SKU specifications.
- Generate vertical video script conforming to `.agents/rules/video-guidelines.md`:
  - `00:00 – 00:03`: Pattern-interrupt visual hook + curiosity line.
  - `00:03 – 00:18`: Problem agitation & relatable story (2.5s scene cuts).
  - `00:18 – 00:28`: Product solution & macro feature showcase.
  - `00:28 – 00:33`: Clear conversion CTA.

### 2. Storyboard & Scene Prompts
- Generate visual storyboard prompts optimized for AI video generation engines (e.g. Google Veo, Runway):
  - Camera angles: Macro cinematic close-up, dynamic tracking shot, clean overhead flat lay.
  - Lighting: Natural soft-box diffused lighting, moody ambient glow.
  - Safe-zone compliance: Keeping all action within central 960x1320 safe box.

### 3. Voiceover & Audio Spec
- Produce formatted voiceover script with timing annotations.
- Provide background audio suggestions (BPM, mood, trending audio tags).
