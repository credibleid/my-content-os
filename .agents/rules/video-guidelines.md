# Video Guidelines — Short-Form Vertical Video (Reels & TikTok 9:16)

## 1. Canvas & Aspect Ratio
- **Resolution:** `1080 x 1920 px` (9:16 vertical ratio).
- **Target Duration:** 30–45 seconds optimal (MVP sweet spot: 33s).

---

## 2. Safe Margins & Interface Overlay Boundaries

TikTok and Instagram Reels have heavy interface overlays (profile icons, audio labels, like/comment buttons, caption box).

- **Top Safe Zone:** Keep top `220px` clear (leave room for search bar, follow buttons, platform tabs).
- **Bottom Safe Zone:** Keep bottom `380px` clear of essential text (captions, hashtags, audio titles sit here).
- **Right Margin Safe Zone:** Keep right `160px` clear (action buttons: like, comment, bookmark, share).
- **Left Margin Safe Zone:** Minimum `60px` padding from left border.
- **Center Focus Zone:** Keep all critical visual hooks, dynamic captions, and product cutouts strictly inside the central `960 x 1320 px` box.

---

## 3. Pacing & Narrative Blueprint (33-Second Sweet Spot)

| Timestamp | Phase | Objective | Visual & Audio Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **00:00 – 00:03** | **Hook** | Interrupt scroll pattern | Rapid visual change, bold center subtitle, polarizing question or controversial statement. |
| **00:03 – 00:18** | **Agitation & Story** | Build deep resonance | Show the relatable annoyance or clumsy traditional solution. Fast jump cuts every 2–2.5s. |
| **00:18 – 00:28** | **The Solution / Showcase** | Seamless product transition | Introduce SKU in action. Show tactile close-up, sound design, zero-friction benefit. |
| **00:28 – 00:33** | **Clear Action (CTA)** | Drive engagement / sales | "Check link in bio" or "Comment [KEYWORD] for link". Direct, frictionless. |

---

## 4. Voiceover & Subtitle Rules
- **Subtitles:** High-contrast text with background box or text-shadow. Max 3–4 words on screen simultaneously.
- **Voiceover Tone:** Conversational, confident, punchy. Avoid monotone AI announcer inflection. Match brand voice defined in `brands/<brand-slug>/profile.md`.
